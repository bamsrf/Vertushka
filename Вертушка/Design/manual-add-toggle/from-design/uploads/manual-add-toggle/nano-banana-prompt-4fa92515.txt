A clean iOS app UI mockup sheet on a light off-white (#F4F5F7) background,
showing ONE floating interactive control in THREE horizontal states,
left to right.

Shared style: minimal, soft, Apple-like. Rounded pill shapes, subtle drop
shadows, thin 1px light-grey strokes. Deep-navy (#23244D) accents. Match the
attached vinyl reference image exactly for the record look (concentric grey
grooves, deep-navy center label reading "ВЕРТУШКА" with tiny "33 1/3 RPM",
black center hole, soft highlight top-left).

STATE 1 — "Collapsed FAB": a single circular floating button, 56px, bottom-right
of a phone screen corner. Inside it the vinyl record from the reference, grey,
with a faint motion-blur arc suggesting slow spinning. Soft shadow under it.

STATE 2 — "Expanded toggle": the same vinyl now sits as the RIGHT-side KNOB of a
horizontal pill toggle (like the attached iOS day/night switch). Pill is
light-grey (#ECEDF0), ~220px wide, 64px tall. On the LEFT of the pill, dark-grey
bold text "Добавить вручную". The vinyl knob is now COLORED — a vibrant RED
vinyl (#E53935) keeping the deep-navy center label. Knob rests at the right end.

STATE 3 — "Activated / sliding": same pill, the colored vinyl knob slid to the
LEFT end of the track with a speed motion-blur trail and small spin lines, as if
it rolled across. The text "Добавить вручную" is partly covered/faded. A faint
upward glow hints a new screen opening.

Evenly spaced, each labeled underneath in small grey caption:
"1 · idle"   "2 · tap"   "3 · slide → open".
Flat vector mockup, no photo background, no device frame, crisp, designer
presentation style.
